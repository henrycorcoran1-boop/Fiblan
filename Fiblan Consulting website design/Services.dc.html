Services.

Every service is delivered by senior engineers directly — scaled to fit a single renovation or a multi-phase development.

01

Project management

A single point of accountability across the full lifecycle of a project — coordinating designers, contractors and suppliers so cost, quality and programme stay aligned.

02

Cost estimating & quantity surveying

Reliable budgets from early concept through to final account — take-offs, tender analysis, valuations and cost control that keep a project on budget, not just on paper.

03

Programme & scheduling

Realistic construction programmes, sequencing and progress tracking — so delays are spotted early, and every party works to the same dates.

04

Site supervision & contract administration

Regular, hands-on presence on site — inspecting works, administering the building contract, and coordinating between contractor, designer and client.

05

Feasibility & planning support

Pre-construction studies, site appraisals and technical input to planning applications — so decisions are made before money is committed.

06

Health, safety & compliance advisory

Regulatory compliance, risk assessment and safety oversight through design and construction — reducing exposure for client and contractor alike.

Scaled to the job.

Not every project needs all six services. Most start with one — a cost estimate, a feasibility review, a single site inspection — and grow from there as trust builds. We scope only what you need.
