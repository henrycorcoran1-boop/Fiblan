A wide reachback network, on call.

Fiblan Consulting is built on a deep bench of highly skilled professionals — across design, commercial and project management — drawn in as and when each project needs them, not carried as permanent overhead.

Built on real sites.

Fiblan's reachback spans quantity surveyors, schedulers, designers, site managers and commercial specialists — a bench far wider than any single in-house team, ready to mobilise on projects from residential extensions to large-scale developments.

We started Fiblan because too many projects — especially smaller ones — go without proper oversight, and end up costing more in rework than the right specialist would ever have cost upfront.

The right people, on demand.

Rather than a fixed bench, we draw on a vetted network of design, commercial and project management professionals — assembled around your project, then stood down once the need has passed. You pay for expertise, not idle capacity.

A few principles.

A team assembled per project

You get the specialists your project actually needs, coordinated by the same lead throughout — never a generic account team.

Honest scoping

A clear fee for a clear scope of work — no open-ended time-and-materials billing.

Independent advice

We're engaged by you, not the contractor — our judgement is only ever in your interest.

Present on site

Problems get caught in person, on site — not from a spreadsheet at a desk.

Let's see if we're a fit.

Tell us what you're building. No pressure, no sales pitch — just an honest answer.
