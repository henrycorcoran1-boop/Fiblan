How we work.

A clear path from your problem to a delivered project — with you involved at every step, and no surprises about scope, timeline or cost.

01

Understand

You describe the project. We visit the site where possible and ask detailed questions about scope, budget and constraints before recommending anything.

02

Scope & estimate

We put together a clear budget and programme, and a fixed fee for our involvement — no open-ended billing.

03

Deliver

We manage the work on site and through the programme — regular reporting, and direct access to the engineer running your project at every stage.

04

Close out & support

We see the project through to final account and handover, and stay available for advice long after we're formally off the job.

Timelines depend entirely on project scope — from a single site visit to multi-year programmes.

Independent, always.

We're engaged directly by you — never by the contractor. That keeps our recommendations on cost, quality and safety squarely in your interest, whether the project is a single renovation or a full development programme.

What you bring.

A clear description of the project and its constraints.

Site access for inspections and reviews as the work progresses.

A willingness to act on our recommendations, even when they're not what you expected to hear.

A decision-maker available for calls on scope, cost and programme.

You don't need construction experience — that's our job. You just need to be clear on what success looks like.

Ready to start?

Describe the project. We'll ask the right questions and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
