How we build software.

A clear path from your problem to working software — with you involved at every step, and no surprises about scope, timeline or cost.

01

Understand

You describe the problem. We ask detailed questions about workflows, pain points, current tools and team size. No assumptions, no jumping to a solution before we understand the work.

02

Design

We sketch solutions — wireframes and flow diagrams. You give feedback. We iterate until we're aligned on exactly what we're building, before a line of production code is written.

03

Build

We build the software, and you see progress every week. Changes can be made as we go; scope is managed openly. You always know where the project stands.

04

Deploy & support

The software goes live. We provide training and ongoing support, fix what needs fixing, and evolve features based on how the tool performs in real-world use.

Most projects take three to six months, depending on complexity.

Built on solid foundations.

We use modern, proven technology: React and Next.js for interfaces, Node.js or Python on the back end, PostgreSQL for data, and AWS or similar cloud infrastructure. We favour open standards and avoid vendor lock-in, so your software is built to last and to evolve.

What you bring.

A clear description of the problem and the workflows around it.

Access to your team for interviews and feedback as we design and build.

A willingness to iterate — we show work early and often.

A decision-maker available for the key calls on scope, timeline and budget.

You don't need to be technical — that's our job. You just need to know your workflow and be clear about what success looks like.

Ready to start?

Describe the problem. We'll ask the right questions and tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
